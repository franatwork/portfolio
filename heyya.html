Heyya.

Heyya is a university club located at the University of Ottawa that seeks to promote and celebrate middle eastern and north african women at campus.

Client
uOttawa Heyya
Role
Brand Designer
Year
2025
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Figma

The brief.

This project sought out to create a visual identity for the club to use as their branding system.

The work.

The brand also seeks to bring strong MENA themes, while looking visually clean and modern. Furthermore, it was important to keep in mind the theme of the client, as so elements representing MENA women were included.
