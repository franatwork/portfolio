Branding — Graphic Design — 2024

ARK Lab.

For this course project, we were tasked with creating an overall concept for an “archive solution” for the university.

Client
IADE — Universidade Europeia
Role
Brand Designer
Year
2024
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign

The brief.

A way in which the university can catalogue and inventory student projects throughout the years, creating a cultural cache that embodies the university and enables students to view what those who came before them created, as well as for teachers to understand subjects.

The work.

My part in this project was to create our team’s branding and visual identity. My rationale was to mimic the campus’ iconic facade, as to create a solid connection to the university. Furthermore, as this would be the last year at this campus, creates an everlasting historical link.
